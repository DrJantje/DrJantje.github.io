Personal home assistance

Mara Home Bridge

A connection between an authorized user's Google Home environment and personal assistant tools.

Mara Home Bridge can retrieve home and device information, inspect device states and supported event history, and send supported device commands requested by the user. Available features depend on Google Home and the connected devices.

Understand your home

Ask about the information and device states that Google Home makes available to your connected assistant.

Request supported controls

Send a device request through the connected assistant. Check the target and reported result, particularly when device data is delayed or unavailable.

Your authorization

Access requires Google authorization from the user. You can revoke future access through Google Account connections or Google Home.

Read the privacy notice and terms of use before connecting. For help, use the support address shown on the application's Google consent screen.
