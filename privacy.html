Data and access

Privacy notice

Mara Home Bridge · Updated October 8, 2026

Information accessed

Mara Home Bridge accesses home and device information made available through the user's Google authorization. This may include home and room identifiers, device names and capabilities, current device states, supported event history, and command results.

How the information is used

The information helps answer the user's requests, identify the intended device, carry out supported home controls, and report the resulting state or command outcome.

The local bridge

When the local Windows bridge is used, persistent OAuth authorization material is stored locally using Windows CurrentUser encryption. Short-lived access tokens are cached in memory.

The local bridge also keeps capability metadata and action attempt records used to identify devices, explain outcomes, and prevent repeated actions. These records may include identifiers, request hashes, timestamps, and outcomes. A fixed automatic deletion schedule for these local records has not been confirmed.

Use through ChatGPT

When the connection is used through ChatGPT, requested home information and commands pass between Google and the OpenAI service hosting that connection. Google and OpenAI process that information under their applicable policies.

The local bridge's encrypted credential storage does not describe ChatGPT's credential storage. This notice does not establish a separate retention period for ChatGPT connector data or conversations.

See Google's privacy policy and OpenAI's privacy policy for their processing practices.

Revocation and stored records

You can revoke future Google access through Google Account connections or Google Home. Revocation does not itself delete information already stored locally or in an assistant conversation.

For questions about local records or requests to remove them, use the support address shown on the application's Google consent screen. No automatic deletion or completed deletion is promised by this notice.

This website

These app-information pages contain no household inventory, device history, camera material, or credentials. They provide no Google sign-in form or device controls.
