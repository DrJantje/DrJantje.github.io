Terms of use

Mara Home Bridge · Updated October 8, 2026

Authorized personal use

Use Mara Home Bridge only with a Google Home environment you are authorized to access. Review device targets and commands, and inform other household members when an assistant can access shared home data or control shared devices.

Availability and results

The connection depends on Google Home, the assistant client, connectivity, and device capabilities. Information may be delayed, unavailable, or incomplete. A command being sent does not always establish that the intended device changed successfully.

Check reported results and keep the manufacturer's controls and suitable supervision for consequential actions.

Discontinue use

You can stop using the connection and revoke future Google access through Google Account connections or Google Home. See the privacy notice for the distinction between revoking access and removing stored records.

Support

Use the support address shown on the application's Google consent screen for questions about access, the local bridge, or these terms.
